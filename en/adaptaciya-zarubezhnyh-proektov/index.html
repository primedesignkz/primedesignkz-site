Что мы предлагаем

Three key areas of work with foreign projects — from documentation rework to consulting support at all stages of approval in Kazakhstan.

01

Adaptation of design solutions

We analyze the provided design documentation and adapt it to current requirements and the regulatory framework of Kazakhstan. We work with architectural, structural and engineering solutions to ensure compliance with local requirements.

02

Supervision государственной экспертизы

We help prepare the project for state expert review and support the interaction with the expert organization. When comments are received, we participate in their analysis, provide clarifications and make corrections to the design documentation.

03

Local design expert review

Our specialists help foreign clients understand the specifics of design and approval of facilities in Kazakhstan, so that foreign design solutions can be correctly implemented in accordance with local requirements.

03

How adaptation works

Standard workflow — from receiving foreign documentation to obtaining a positive conclusion from RK expert review.

Step 01

Audit документации

Receiving foreign project, assessing completeness and section composition, identifying non-compliance with RK standards. Timeline — 5-10 business days.

Step 02

План адаптации

We form a list of necessary changes, timeline and budget estimate. We coordinate with the client and foreign design team.

Step 03

Section rework

Structure is recalculated for RK seismicity and climate. Engineering is brought to HVAC/water/electrical standards. Formatting — per RK SNiP.

Step 04

Submission for expert review

Document collation, expertise payment, submission to RSE "Gosexpertiza". Work with comments until positive conclusion.

Step 05

Delivery to client

Full set of adapted documentation + positive expert review conclusion. Ready to go to the construction site.

Step 06

Site supervision

On request — support of construction works, participation in acceptance of hidden works, keeping site supervision log.

04

Frequently asked questions

What foreign clients most often ask about the project adaptation procedure in Kazakhstan.

What is foreign project adaptation?

Bringing design documentation developed by foreign architects or design firms into compliance with the current regulations of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

Includes rework of architectural, structural and engineering sections, documentation formatting to Kazakhstan standards and state expert review support.

Who needs the adaptation service?
  • Foreign developers implementing projects in Kazakhstan
  • Foreign investors who purchased a ready facility or project
  • International general contractors working with foreign design documentation
  • Kazakh clients who acquired a ready foreign project
What do you adapt?
  • Architecture — layouts, facades, joints to RK standards
  • Structural design — calculation for Kazakhstan's seismicity and climate
  • Engineering systems — HVAC, water, electrical, low-current per RK standards
  • Master plan — landscaping, planting per Kazakhstan standards
  • Industrial and fire safety — separate sections
How long does adaptation take?

Depends on completeness of source documentation, volume and class of facility. Approximately:

  • Small facility (up to 10,000 m²) — 6–8 недель
  • Medium (10-40,000 m²) — 8-12 weeks
  • Large (over 40,000 m²) — 12-16 weeks

State expert review — additional 30-90 business days.

Which languages do you work in?
  • Русский — main documentation language
  • Казахский — for official documents, expert review, approvals
  • Английский — for interaction with foreign design team
  • On request — Uzbek and Chinese
Who owns the copyright to the adapted project?

The original project remains in the ownership of the foreign client/designer. Prime Design receives rights only for adapted sections necessary for implementation in Kazakhstan. Terms are fixed in the contract.

How much does it cost?

Cost depends on facility area and complexity, completeness of source documentation, required rework scope.

Ориентир: 30-70% of the cost of developing a project "from scratch". Accurate calculation after audit of foreign documentation.
