Class 02

Metallurgy and heavy industry

Reconstruction and new construction of metallurgical plant workshops. Work under conditions of operating productions.

02

Design documentation composition

Sections required for category-1 industrial facilities. In addition to standard architectural and construction sections, technological and special parts are critical for industry.

General building sections

  • АР — architectural solutions for buildings and administrative-domestic blocks
  • RC, SC — reinforced concrete and steel structures, crane runways, crane girders
  • ГП — master plan: technological driveways, sites, railway tracks
  • ПОС — construction organization design considering technological equipment installation

Engineering systems

  • ОВ — ventilation of production premises, aspiration, local exhausts
  • ВК — process water pipes, recycled water supply, industrial sewerage
  • ЭОМ — power electrical equipment, distribution boards, reactive power compensation
  • СС — process automation (APCS), dispatching

Specialty sections

  • ТХ — process solutions: equipment placement, material flows, connection to utilities
  • ПБ — industrial safety declaration, accident risk assessment
  • ООС — environmental impact assessment, emission inventory
  • МОЭ — energy efficiency measures

Industry-specific

For energy — hydraulic structures, machine halls, electrical engineering. For oil & gas — explosion protection, lightning protection, industrial safety specialty sections. For metallurgy — work in operating production conditions, phased commissioning.

Frequently asked questions

What industrial clients most often ask about design specifics, licenses and Prime Design industry experience.

What industrial facilities do you design?

Full range of industrial facilities:

  • Production buildings and workshops
  • Warehouses and logistics complexes
  • Service centers (Komatsu, SITRAK, etc.)
  • Energy facilities — HPPs up to 19 MW, distribution substations
  • Oil & gas infrastructure
  • Metallurgy facility reconstruction

More than 30 industrial facilities delivered in RK and RUz.

Yes ли лицензия на промышленную безопасность?

Yes. Prime Design имеет:

  • Category 1 design activity license (RK and RUz)
  • Certificate for the right to perform works в области industrial safety
  • Survey activity license
  • Technical inspection accreditation

Full list of licenses provided upon request.

Do you work with oil & gas facilities?

Yes. Портфель включает объекты нефтегазовой инфраструктуры, в том числе IGCC Atyrau, oil products storage and transshipment sites.

We work considering industrial safety, explosion protection standards, lightning protection requirements and industry-specific standards.

Do you design HPPs and other energy facilities?

Yes. В портфеле — HPP-1 and HPP-2 on the Kalzhir River with total capacity of 19 MW (EKO), Komatsu facilities, OCS JSC Qarmet.

Comprehensive design includes:

  • Hydraulic structures (water intakes, derivation, water intake chambers)
  • Machine halls with turbine and generator equipment
  • Switchgear and relay protection
  • Administrative and technical infrastructure
What volumes do you take on?

Industrial facilities — from small service centers (2,000 m²) to large productions and energy complexes. Average scale — 10,000-40,000 m² of main buildings.

For reconstruction of existing productions, we take projects of any scale with phased commissioning.

How do you work with the process part?

Process solutions (TH section) are executed by our own process engineers or in close cooperation with the client's equipment suppliers.

Наш подход:

  • At the start — process TOR or equipment list from the client
  • Elaboration of process scheme, material flows, connections
  • Coordination с архитектурой, конструкциями and инженерными сетями
  • Coordination with equipment suppliers on mounting points and connections
