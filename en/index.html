Composition of the working design

Engineering design is a complex of interconnected sections ensuring safe and reliable construction. Each section is developed by a specialist engineer and integrated into a unified digital model.

АР

Architecture

  • Spatial planning solutions
  • Facades, sections, interiors
  • Finishing materials
  • Rooms, joints, details
RC · SC

Structures

  • Foundations
  • Frame (reinforced concrete, steel)
  • Floors and columns
  • Load and seismic calculations
HVAC · Water · Electrical · Low-current · Fire alarm · Auto-extinguishing

Engineering systems

  • Heating, ventilation, air conditioning
  • Water supply and sewerage
  • Power supply and lighting
  • Low-current systems, fire alarm, auto-extinguishing
ГП

Master plan

  • Vertical planning
  • Roads, driveways, parking
  • Site landscaping
  • Small architectural forms
ПОС

Construction organization

  • Works organization design
  • Schedule and network diagrams
  • Cost estimate documentation
  • Explanatory notes
BIM

Unified BIM model

Integration of all sections into a unified information model — early clash detection, accurate quantity takeoff, automated drawing and specification generation.

  • Coordination разделов в Revit
  • Clash detection
  • Automatic drawing and specification generation

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions о проектировании, стадиях, стоимости and работе с Prime Design.

What is full-cycle building design?

Comprehensive design — development of all design and working documentation sections by one company: architecture, structures, engineering systems (HVAC, water, electrical, low-current systems), master plan, construction organization.

All sections are coordinated in a unified BIM model, which eliminates on-site clashes and allows accurate calculation of quantities and schedules.

Which design stages does Prime Design cover?

Full cycle: project concept → sketch design (SD) → working design (WD) → site supervision. Prime Design engages at any stage and takes on the required scope — from initial concept to support of previously issued documentation.

Does Prime Design have design licenses?

Five current documents:

  • Design activity license — Category 1 of the Republic of Kazakhstan
  • Design activity license — Category 1 of the Republic of Uzbekistan
  • Survey activity license (инженерно-геологические изыскания)
  • Certificate for the right to perform works в области industrial safety
  • Technical inspection accreditation (технический and строительный надзор)
Do you work in BIM?

All projects выполняются в BIM-среде. Основной инструмент — Autodesk Revit (Architecture, Structure, MEP). Coordination — в Navisworks. Расчёты — в LIRA-CAD and SCAD Office. Visualization — в 3ds Max.

A unified information model serves as the source of all drawings, specifications and work quantities.

Which facility types does Prime Design design?
  • Residential комплексы — from point development to full-cycle residential complexes
  • Industrial and энергетические объекты: HPPs, gas chemistry, metallurgy, service centers
  • Public buildings: schools (including NIS), sports and cultural facilities
  • Transport and logistics centers and warehouse complexes
  • Commercial объекты: service centers, shopping centers, landscaping

Internal sections:

  • АР — architectural solutions (layouts, facades, sections, joints)

  • ОВ — heating, ventilation, air conditioning
  • ВК — water supply and sewerage
  • ЭОМ — power supply and lighting

  • External heating networks
  • External lighting and low-current networks

  • ГП — master plan and landscaping
  • ПОС — construction organization design and cost estimate documentation

What is site (author) supervision?

Site supervision — control of compliance of works performed on site with the approved project. The main task is to ensure the facility is built strictly according to the agreed design documentation, without unauthorized deviations and simplifications.

Prime Design checks every stage of construction works for compliance with the approved design, records deviations and coordinates necessary corrections. We work on site until commissioning.
