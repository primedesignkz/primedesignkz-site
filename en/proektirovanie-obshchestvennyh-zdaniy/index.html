02

Regulatory framework

Public buildings in Kazakhstan are designed considering numerous industry standards. Prime Design works with the full requirements package for each facility class.

General building codes

  • СНиП РК and СП РК — set of rules and building codes
  • ГОСТ — state standards for materials, structures, engineering
  • Technical regulations "Building and Structure Safety Requirements"

Industry standards

  • Школы: MoE RK standards, NIS requirements (for intellectual schools), MZhS
  • Медицина: sanitary-epidemiological requirements, Ministry of Health standards
  • ТРЦ: fire safety standards (SNiP 2.04.09), evacuation, accessibility
  • Спорткомплексы: FISA/FIFA/IIHF standards for specialized facilities

Expert review

All projects public buildings проходят mandatory comprehensive expert review at RSE "Gosexpertiza". Prime Design supports the expert review until a positive conclusion is obtained.

03

Working design composition

Standard set of design documentation for a public building. Can be supplemented with industry-specific sections (medical technology, sports equipment, retail zoning).

Main sections

  • АР — architectural solutions, facades, layouts, MOP interiors
  • RC, SC — structure, sports complexes often require long-span trusses
  • ОВ, ВК, ЭОМ, СС — engineering systems considering specifics (medical gases, server rooms, catering kitchens)
  • ГП — master plan, parking, landscaping, accessibility
  • ПОС — construction organization
  • ПБ — fire safety section with evacuation calculation

Industry sections

  • Medical technology — for hospitals, clinics, laboratories
  • Retail technology — for malls, SC (zoning, visitor flows)
  • Sports technology — for stadiums, sports complexes (specialized surfaces, equipment)
  • Interior design project — per separate TOR
  • Kitchen technology — for facilities with catering unit

Frequently asked questions

What clients most often ask about the design of schools, malls, business centers and other public facilities.

Do you design schools?

Yes, это одно из наших ключевых направлений. В портфеле — Al-Farabi School (NIS Atyrau) and 2,000-seat school in Semey.

We design both stand-alone schools and schools within residential districts. We work per NIS, SanPiN requirements, MoE RK and MZhS standards.

How much does school design cost?

Ориентировочно 600-1,500 KZT per m² for full design cycle (concept + SD + WD).

For a 1,200-student school (about 15,000 m²) — from 12 to 25 million KZT. Accurate calculation is prepared after filling in questionnaire, 2 business days.

What public buildings do you design?
  • Schools (600-2,000 students), kindergartens, lyceums, gymnasiums
  • Multi-disciplinary hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers
  • Business centerы класса A, B+ (5 000–80 000 м²)
  • Malls, SC, food courts, retail parks
  • Stadiums, sports and health complexes, ice arenas, swimming pools
  • Theaters, community centers, galleries, religious buildings
Do you work per NIS standards?

Yes. Al-Farabi School спроектирована для сети Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools with full compliance with their corporate technical requirements and MZhS standards.
